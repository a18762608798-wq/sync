# 三维中心力场

## General solution

### In math

* Equation

$$
[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}(\partial_r^2+\frac{2}{r}\partial_r)+\frac{\hat l^2}{2\mu r^2}+V( r)]R(r)Y(\theta,\varphi)=ER( r)Y(\theta,\varphi)
$$

* variables separation

Known: $l^2 \psi(r,\theta,\varphi)=\hbar^2l(l+1)\psi(r,\theta,\varphi)$

$$
\{[\frac{p_r^2}{2\mu}+\frac{\hbar^2l(l+1)}{2\mu r^2}]-[E-V(r)]\}R_l(r)=0
$$

这是完美的动能减动能为0，这反映了能量用$R(r)$中的量子数$（n_r,l）$足以完全表示。

* For so called simplify form, we cound make $\chi_l(r)=r_rR(r)$ 实际上只有类氢原子用一用。

$$
\{[\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}\frac{d^2\chi_l}{dr^2}+\frac{\hat l^2}{2\mu r^2}]+[E-V(r)]\}\chi_l=0
$$

### In physics

* Divergent behavior

To sum up, the solution of $\chi_l\propto r^{l+1}\rightarrow0$ meet the condition of schrodinger equation.

## specific case
### 氢原子

自然单位制

* Equation

$$
\begin{cases}
\{[\frac{1}{2}\frac{d^2}{dr^2}+\frac{\hat l^2}{2 r^2}]-[E+\frac{1}{r}]\}R_{n_rl}(r)=0\\
R_{n_rl}(0,+\infty)\rightarrow 0
\end{cases}
$$

Turn the bound

According to $x\rightarrow0$ and $x\rightarrow\infty$

$$
\begin{cases}
\chi_l=r^{l+1}e^{-\beta r}u_l(r)\\
\beta=\sqrt{-2E}\\
\end{cases}
$$

Then we can get the Confluent hypergeometric equation

$$
confluent(\alpha=l+1-\frac{1}{\beta}=-n_r,\gamma=2(l+1)\ge2,u(\xi=2\beta r))
$$

* Solution

1. Eigenvalue

$$
E=-\frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{n_r+l+1})^2=-\frac{1}{2n^2}\Rightarrow-\frac{e^2}{2an^2}
$$

2. Wavefuction

$$
R_{nl}(r)=\frac{\chi}{r}=r^le^{-r/n}F(\alpha,\gamma,\xi)\propto \xi^le^{-\xi/n}F(\alpha,\gamma,\xi)\Rightarrow (\xi/a)^le^{-\xi/an}F(-n_r,\gamma,\xi/a) \propto \xi^le^{-\xi/n}F(-n_r,\gamma,\xi/a)
$$

### 球方势阱

#### Infinite potential

* Equation

$$
\{[\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}\frac{d^2}{d r^2}+\frac{\hat l^2}{2\mu r^2}]-E\}R_l(r)=0
$$

* Construct the form

$$
Bessel(m,R(\rho=kr))
$$

* SolutionEigenfunction

$$
R_l(\rho)=C_lJ_l(\rho)=C_lJ_l(kr)
$$

2. Eigenvalue

Think of Boundary: $R(ka)=0$

Assume the solution is $ka=x_{n_rl}$

$$
E=\frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2\mu}=\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu a^2}x_{n_rl}^2\quad(n_r,l\in\mathbb{N})
$$

#### limited potential

* Solution

1. Eigenfunction

For bound state:

$$
\begin{cases}
R_l(\rho)=C_1j_l(kr)\quad(r<a)\\
R_l(\rho)=C_2h_l(k'r)\quad(r>a,k'\quad is\quad imaginary)
\end{cases}
$$

2. Eigenvalue

Think of boundary's continous. We only get numerical solution

### 各向同性谐振子

* Equation

$$
\begin{cases}
\{[\frac{1}{2}\frac{d^2}{dr^2}+\frac{\hat l^2}{2 r^2}]-[E-\frac{1}{2}r^2]\}R_{n_rl}(r)=0\\
R_{n_rl}(0,+\infty)\rightarrow r^l,0
\end{cases}
$$

* Construct this form

Think of $r\rightarrow 0,\infty$ :

$$
R_l(r)=r^l\exp(-r^2/2)u_l(r)
$$

Then we can get the Confluent hypergeometric equation

$$
confluent(\alpha=\frac{1}{2}(l+3/2-E)=-n_r,\gamma=l+3/2\ne\mathbb Z,u(\xi=r^2))
$$

* Solution

1. Eigenfunction

Think of $u=c_1y_1+c_2y_2=c_1y_1=c_1F(\alpha,\gamma,\xi)$

$$
R_{n_rl}(r)=r^l\exp(-r^2/2)F(\alpha,\gamma,\xi)\Rightarrow r^l\exp(-r^2/2x_0^2)F(-n_r,\gamma,\xi/x_0^2)
$$

2. Eigenvalue

$$
E=2n_r+l+3/2=N+3/2\Rightarrow\hbar w(N+3/2)
$$
