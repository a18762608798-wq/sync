# 二维一维中心立场

## 二维中心力场

### General solution

* premise

The common consered quantities of the system: $[\hat l^2,\hat l_z]$ ,the common eigenstate is that

$$
\psi(\rho,\theta)=R_m(\rho)e^{im\varphi}
$$

* equation

So the equation of radial direction we could write as

$$
\{[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}(\frac{d^2}{d\rho^2}+\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{d}{d\rho})+\frac{\hat l_z^2}{2\mu r^2}]-[E-V(\rho)]\}R_{n|m|}=0
$$

If we let $R_{n|m|}=\chi_{n|m|}/\sqrt \rho$ in anticipation of comparing $\chi_{nm}$ of dim3

$$
\{-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}[\frac{d^2}{d\rho^2}-\frac{(m-1/2)(m+1/2)}{\rho^2}]-[E-V(\rho)]\}\chi_{n|m|}=0
$$

Then we could get the sound relationship

$$
l(l+1)\approx(m-1)(m+1)\rightarrow l\approx |m|-1/2
$$

### specific cases

#### Infinite deep round potential well for dim2

甚至不用自然单位制

* Equation and boundary

$$
\begin{cases}
\{[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu}(\frac{d^2}{d\rho^2}+\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{d}{d\rho})+\frac{\hat l_z^2}{2\mu r^2}]-E\}R_{n|m|}=0\\
R_{n|m|}(\rho=a)=0\\
R_{n|m|}(\rho=0)=?
\end{cases}
$$

Turn the variable

$$
Bessel(m,\xi=k\rho)
$$

* Solution

Think of the boundary$R_{n|m|}(\rho\rightarrow\infty)\rightarrow 0$

$$
R_{n|m|}(\xi)=C_1J_m+C_2J_{-m}\rightarrow J_{|m|}(\xi=k\rho)
$$

问题: $J_{n|m|}$ 完备吗？

答：完备，因为只有一个有效的边界条件。

#### 二维各项同性谐振子

自然单位制

* Equation and bound

$$
\begin{cases}
\{[-\frac{1}{2}(\frac{d^2}{d\rho^2}+\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{d}{d\rho})+\frac{\hat l_z^2}{2\rho^2}]-[E-\frac{1}{2}\rho^2]\}R(\rho)=\\
R(\rho)\propto\rho^{|m|}e^{-\rho^2/2}u(\rho) \quad(bound)
\end{cases}
$$

Turn the variable

$$
confluent(\alpha=\frac{|m|+1}{2}-\frac{E}{2}=-n_\rho,\gamma=|m|+1,\xi=\rho^2)
$$

* Solution

Think of $\gamma\ge1$ , $u=C_1y_1$ is meet

$$
u_{n_\rho |m|}(\rho)=F(-n_r,|m|+1,\rho^2)\rightarrow F(-n_r,|m|+1,\rho^2/x_0^2)
$$

#### 二维氢原子

 自然单位制

* Equation and bound

$$
\begin{cases}
\{[-\frac{1}{2}(\frac{d^2}{d\rho^2}+\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{d}{d\rho})+\frac{\hat l_z^2}{2\rho^2}]-[E+\frac{1}{\rho}]\}R(\rho)=0\\
R(\rho)\propto e^{|m|}e^{-\beta\rho}u(\rho)\\
\beta=\sqrt{-2E}\quad (bound)
\end{cases}
$$

Turn the variable

$$
confluent(\alpha=|m|+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{\beta}=-n_{r},\gamma=2|m|+1,\xi=2\beta\rho)
$$

* Solution

$$
u_{n_\rho|m|}(\rho)=F(-n_r,2|m|+1,2\beta\rho)\rightarrow F(-n_r,2|m|+1,2\beta\rho/a)
$$

#### 一维氢原子

* Premise

The potential is $V(x)=V(-x)=-\frac{e^2}{|x|}$ ,if make $|x|\rightarrow r,r\ge0$ . we can construct the solution.

* Equation and bound

It is similar as l=0 of dim3

$$
\begin{cases}
\chi_0''+2(E+\frac{1}{r})\chi_0=0\\
R(0,+\infty)\rightarrow0,0
\end{cases}
$$

* Solution

$$
\begin{cases}
F_0\quad(x>0)\\
\psi_+=\begin{cases}
xe^{-x/n}F_0(x)\\
-xe^{x/n}F_0(-x)
\end{cases}\\
\psi_-=\begin{cases}
xe^{-x/n}F_0(x)\\
xe^{x/n}F_0(-x)
\end{cases}
\end{cases}
$$

注意，基态由于0处连续性十分复杂，不讨论。
