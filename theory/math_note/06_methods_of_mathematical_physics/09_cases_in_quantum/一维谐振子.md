# 一维谐振子

* Equation

$$
(-\frac{1}{2}\frac{d^2}{dx^2}+\frac{1}{2}x^2)\psi(x)=E\psi(x)
$$

* Construct the form

$$
\begin{cases}
\psi(x)=e^{-\frac{1}{2}x^2}u(x)\\
Hermite [u(x),\lambda=2E]
\end{cases}
$$

* Truncation conditiom

$$
\lambda-1=2E-1=2n\quad (n\in \mathbb{N})
$$

meaning:

$$
E=(n+\frac{1}{2})\Rightarrow\hbar w(n+\frac{1}{2})\quad(n\in\mathbb{N})
$$

* Wavefunction

$$
\psi_n(x)=e^{-\frac{1}{2}x^2}u_n(x)=e^{-\frac{1}{2}x^2}H_n(x)\Rightarrow e^{-\frac{1}{2} (x/x_0)^2}H(x/x_0)\\
x_0=\sqrt{\hbar/\mu w_0 }
$$
