# 球坐标系下$[l^2,l_z]$ 的共同本征态

* Equation

$$
\begin{cases}
\hat l^2Y(\theta,\varphi)=\hbar^2 \lambda Y(\theta,\varphi)\\
\hat l^2=-\frac{\hbar^2}{\sin\theta}\partial_\theta(\sin\theta\partial_\theta)+\frac{\hat l_z^2}{\sin^2\theta}(目前这个没有简单得到的方法)
\end{cases}
$$

* variables separation

Let $Y(\theta,\varphi)=\Theta(\theta)\Phi(\varphi)$ ,$\hat l_z Y=m\hbar Y$ .

$$
\frac{1}{\sin\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta}(\sin\theta\frac{d\Theta}{d\theta})+(\lambda-\frac{m^2}{\sin^2\theta})\Theta=0 \quad(\sin\theta\in[-1,1])
$$

* Solution

1. Truncation condition

Associated is as same as Legend's function

$$
\begin{cases}
\lambda=l(l+1)\quad(l\in \mathbb{N})\\
|m|\leq l \quad(m\in\mathbb{Z})
\end{cases}
$$

2. Eigenvalue and eigenfunction

$$
\begin{cases}
\hbar^2\lambda=\hbar^2l(l+1)\\
Y=P_l^m(\cos\theta)
\end{cases}
$$
