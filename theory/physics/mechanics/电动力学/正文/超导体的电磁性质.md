# 超导体的电磁性质

## 基本性质

* 超导电性。
* 迈斯纳效应：只有表面有磁场，其他磁场进不去。
* 相变曲线：H（I）-T图像，封闭空间内为超导态。注意第二类超导体不止一个临界磁场。

* 分类：第一、二类超导体。分别为单一态和混合态。

## 唯象理论

### 伦敦第一方程

#### Hypothesize

* $J=J_f+J_s$, $J_s$ is superconducting electric current.

* The superconducting electric particle is conform  to $m\frac{\part v}{\part t}=-eE$

#### Conclusion

* $\frac{\part J_s}{\part t}=-n_se\frac{\part v}{\part t}=\alpha E=0$

$$
\begin{cases}
\frac{\part J_s}{\part t}=\alpha E\\
\alpha=\frac{n_se^2}{m}
\end{cases}
$$



* so $E=0$, $J_f=\sigma E=0$, $\sigma$ have no effect. ==Meaning there is no resistance in constant==

#### Problem

* $\nabla\times E=-\frac{\part B}{\part t}=0$, so $B$ is constant, but we can not get  **Meissner effect**

### The local relationship between superconducting current and vector-potential

#### London's norm

* We have norm $\nabla\cdot A=0$ aim to $\nabla\cdot(A'-\nabla \psi)=0$, $A’=A+\nabla\psi$; We still can not confirm $A$。

​		No we norm ==$A\vert_{s}=0$==
​		$\oint_sAdS=\int_S(A'-\nabla\psi)dS=\int_V\nabla\cdot(A'-\nabla\psi)=\int_V(\nabla\cdot A'-\Delta\psi)dV=\int_V(\nabla\cdot A')dV=\oint_s A'\vert_sdS$

​		Compare the last term and the first term $A=A'\vert_s$, therefore $\nabla\psi\vert_s=0$,  with$\Delta\psi=0$, meaning:

$$
\begin{cases}
\psi=C\\
A=A'

$$

### Pipaddt non-local revision

#### Demand

* The real $\lambda_L$ is longer than calculation. 

* The effect is non-local.

* The effect have some relationship with **mean free path**.

#### Hypothesize

* $J_s(x)=-\frac{3a}{4\pi\xi_0}\int_V\frac{r[r\cdot A(x')]e^{-\frac{r}{\xi_p}}}{r^4}dV'$

* $\frac{1}{\xi_p}=\frac{1}{\xi_0}+\frac{1}{dl}$

#### The statement of symbols

* $\xi_p$ 有效相关长度

* $\xi_0$ 金属超导体相关长度

* $d$ 材料系数

* $l$ 平均自由程
* $\lambda_p$ 有效穿透深度

#### ==Specially ideal==

*  Theory

​		When
$$
\begin{cases}
J_s(x)=-\frac{\alpha}{\xi_0}\xi_pA(x)=-\alpha'A(x)\\
\lambda_p=\lambda_L(\frac{\xi_0}{\xi_p})^{\frac{1}{2}}\approx\lambda_L(\frac{\xi_0}{dl})^{\frac{1}{2}}
\end{cases}\quad  dl\ll\xi_0, \xi_p\rightarrow ,dl \xi_p\ll\lambda_p,
$$


* Statement
  1. $\xi_p\ll\lambda_p$ is belongs to **Second kind superconductivity**.
  2. If $dl=0$, mean we ignore the effect of mean free path, we will get $\lambda_p=\lambda_L$, we will come back to London equation.
