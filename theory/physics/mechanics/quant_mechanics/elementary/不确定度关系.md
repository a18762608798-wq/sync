# 不确定度关系

Ref to [@Noauthor-d, 4]

给出一种精巧的不确定度关系的证明:

## the relationship between $\Delta F$ and $\delta F$

Definte:

$$
\begin{split}
&\Delta F = \sqrt{f_2 - f^2} \\
&\delta F = F - f
\end{split}
$$

where

$$
\begin{cases}
f_2 = \langle F^2\rangle \\
f = \langle F \rangle
\end{cases}
$$

因此有:

$$
\langle\delta F\rangle^2
= \langle (F - f)^\dagger (F - f)\rangle
= f_2 - f^2 = (\Delta F )^2
$$

同时有:

$$
(\Delta F)^2 = \langle \delta F \rangle^2 = ||\delta F |\psi\rangle ||^2 \ge 0
$$

显然 $\Delta F$ 是 real 且是 sqrt 形式所以有:

$$
\Delta F \ge 0
$$

## 方差与不确定度:量子-经典对应

$(\Delta F)^2 = \langle F^2\rangle - \langle F\rangle^2$ 在形式上就是经典概率论的方差
$\mathrm{Var}(X) = E(X^2) - E(X)^2$,只是期望改用 Born 规则
$\langle F\rangle = \langle\psi|F|\psi\rangle$ (混合态则为 $\mathrm{Tr}(\rho F)$).
因此单次测量结果的统计涨落,完全按经典方差理解即可;量子特色不在此式本身,
而在不对易量不能同时取零,以及期望值对态的依赖方式上.

单比特是最干净的例子.对 $\sigma_z$ 有 $\sigma_z^2 = I$,故 $\langle\sigma_z^2\rangle \equiv 1$,
与态无关,方差只剩第二项:

$$
(\Delta\sigma_z)^2 = 1 - \langle\sigma_z\rangle^2.
$$

这正是取值为 $\pm 1$ 的 Bernoulli 变量的方差.取 Bloch 球纯态
$|\psi\rangle = \cos\frac{\theta}{2}|+\rangle_z + e^{i\phi}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}|-\rangle_z$,
有 $\langle\sigma_z\rangle = \cos\theta$,于是

$$
(\Delta\sigma_z)^2 = \sin^2\theta,\quad \Delta\sigma_z = |\sin\theta|.
$$

## Robertson uncertainty relation

According to **cauchy inequality**:

$$
|\langle \phi | \chi \rangle |^2
\le \langle \phi | \phi \rangle \langle \chi | \chi \rangle
$$

Thus

$$
|\langle \delta A \delta B \rangle|^2
\le \langle (\delta A)^2 \rangle \langle (\delta B)^2 \rangle
= (\Delta A)^2 (\Delta B)^2
$$

Also

$$
\langle \delta A \delta B \rangle
= \frac{1}{2}\langle \{\delta A, \delta B\} \rangle
+ \frac{1}{2}\langle [\delta A, \delta B]\rangle
$$

注意第一项是厄迷算符, 期望是实数；第二项和厄米算符相差i, 期望是虚数.
因此模方是两项模方和.

So

$$
|\langle \delta A \delta B \rangle|^2
= \frac{1}{4} |\langle\{ \delta A, \delta B\}\rangle|^2
+ \frac{1}{4} |\langle[\delta A, \delta B]\rangle|^2
$$

In summary,

$$
(\Delta A)^2(\Delta B)^2
\ge \frac{1}{4} |\langle[\delta A, \delta B]\rangle|^2
$$

since $\Delta A, \Delta B$ is real, there are

$$
\Delta A \Delta B \ge \frac{1}{2}|\langle[\delta A, \delta B]\rangle|
$$
