Basic on: 
$$
\frac{d}{dt} (\frac{\partial T}{\partial \dot q_\alpha}) - \frac{\partial T}{\partial q_\alpha} = Q_\alpha
$$
For conservative force: $Q_\alpha = - \frac{\partial V}{\partial q_\alpha}$
Notice: $\frac{\partial V}{\partial \dot q_\alpha} = 0$
Therefore:
$$
\frac{d}{dt}(\frac{\partial L}{\partial \dot q_\alpha}) - \frac{\partial L}{\partial q_\alpha} = 0
$$
QED.
